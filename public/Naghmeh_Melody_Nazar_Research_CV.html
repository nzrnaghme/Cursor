M.S. Thesis — Speech Emotion Recognition, CSUN

2024–2026
  • Developed a 2D ResNet-inspired CNN for neutral, happy, sad, and angry speech labels using RAVDESS and TESS.
  • Processed speech at 16 kHz into 128 × 128 log-Mel spectrograms and trained with Adam, dropout, and residual connections.
  • On the current split, achieved 80.36% validation accuracy and 74.4% on a held-out mixed test set.
  • Speaker-independent and cross-corpus performance, desktop latency, and real-world generalization remain to be evaluated; no clinical validation is claimed.
